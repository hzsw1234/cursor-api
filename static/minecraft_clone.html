Three.js powered / single file / procedural world

A pocket voxel world in one html file.

Explore an endless-feeling handcrafted wilderness: mine stone, harvest wood, place blocks, craft essentials, dodge zombies, meet wandering animals, and watch the sky bend from sunrise gold to moonlit blue.

Terrain

Layered noise sculpts hills, caves, beaches, rivers, cliffs, plains, deserts, taiga, and meadows.

Pixel materials

Every block texture is painted procedurally on a tiny canvas atlas for a crisp retro look.

Creatures

Pigs, sheep, cows, slimes, and zombies roam or hunt with simple state-based behavior.

Survival loop

Gather, craft, build, heal, sprint, fight, and persist your progress in local storage.

Start a world

Any text becomes a reproducible terrain seed.
Higher settings load more chunks and richer skylines.

Survival Notes

  • Logs craft into planks, then sticks, torches, and stronger building pieces.
  • Zombies emerge in darkness. Keep moving or build walls before night.
  • Slimes roam damp lowlands and drop extra experience when defeated.

Quality of life

World state, changed blocks, day time, player stats, and inventory persist to local storage under your chosen seed.
